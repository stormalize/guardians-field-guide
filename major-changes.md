This page lists major changes or additions from each expansion that have had a substantial impact across the game and are still relevant as of the <cite>Monument of Triumph</cite> update. Detailed sandbox balance notes, economy changes, and individual new content introduced with an expansion (campaigns, gear, maps, etc.) are _not_ included.

Notable changes introduced during a Seasonal update that followed an expansion are grouped together with the rest of the expansion notes.

[Full Destiny Patch Notes Archive](https://destiny.bungie.org/patchnotes/)

<h2 variant="justify"><img m="icon" src="/assets/images/icons/extra/expansion-forsaken.png"> Forsaken <span part="suffix"><span style="opacity: 0.6;">v2.0.0 / </span>September 2018</span></h2>

- <span class="fmt-label">New</span> **Collections** tab - keep track of all of the items and gear you have unlocked

- <span class="fmt-label">New</span> **Gear**: <i>Combat Bow</i> weapon type, <i>Machine Gun</i> weapon type
- <span class="fmt-label">New</span> **Subclasses**: Additional Super abilities for all Subclasses
- <span class="fmt-label" style="background-color: var(--m-color--accent-4);">Updated</span> **Gear**: Reintroduce Special weapons into Kinetic and Energy slots

<h2 variant="justify"><img m="icon" src="/assets/images/icons/expansion-shadowkeep.png"> Shadowkeep <span part="suffix"><span style="opacity: 0.6;">v2.6.0 / </span>October 2019</span></h2>

- <span class="fmt-label">New</span> **Seasonal <a href="/manual/#artifacts">Artifact</a> Slot**
- <span class="fmt-label">New</span> **Combat Systems**: <a href="/manual/#finishers">Finishers</a>
- <span class="fmt-label">New</span> **Enemies**: <a href="/manual/#champions">Champions</a>
- <span class="fmt-label">New</span> **Gear**: Armor 2.0 with stat rework and fully customizable bonuses via Mods (existing armor _not_ updated)
- <span class="fmt-label">New</span> **Gear**: <i>Wave Frame</i> Grenade Launchers, <i>Caster Frame</i> Swords, <i>Vortex Frame</i> Swords
- <span class="fmt-label">New</span> **Game Mode**: Trials of Osiris
- <span class="fmt-label">New</span> **<a href="/manual/#appearance-customization">Universal Ornaments</a>** - Cosmetic items to change the appearance of any Legendary Armor
- <span class="fmt-label" style="background-color: var(--m-color--accent-4);">Updated</span> **Gear**: Weapon Mods are now permanent unlocks and no longer consumable
- <span class="fmt-label" style="background-color: var(--m-color--accent-4);">Updated</span> **Gear**: Sword rework that adds energy for blocking/heavy attacks and revised stats
- <span class="fmt-label" style="background-color: var(--m-color--accent-4);">Updated</span> **Combat Systems**: Visual damage number crunch for better readability
- <span class="fmt-label" style="background-color: var(--m-color--accent-4);">Updated</span> **Controls**: Fully custom controller layouts

<h2 variant="justify"><img m="icon" src="/assets/images/icons/expansion-beyond-light.png"> Beyond Light <span part="suffix"><span style="opacity: 0.6;">v3.0.0 / </span>November 2020</span></h2>

- <span class="fmt-label">New</span> **Subclasses**: Stasis
- <span class="fmt-label">New</span> **Gear**: <i>Aggressive Frame</i> Fusion Rifles
- <span class="fmt-label">New</span> **Cross Play** - Enable matchmaking and friends list for Guardians across PC/Xbox/Playstation
- <span class="fmt-label">New</span> **<a href="/manual/#appearance-customization">Appearance Customization</a>** - New unified screen to apply Ornaments and Shaders to all armor at once, add ability to convert any owned armor into a Universal Ornament
- <span class="fmt-label">New</span> **New Light Tutorial** - Overhaul of player onboarding, including the return of the Cosmodrome
- <span class="fmt-label">New</span> **Monument to Lost Light** - Tower vendor that offers older Exotic Weapons
- <span class="fmt-label">New</span> **Destiny Timeline** - New section in the Director that lists all expansions and seasons in release order
- <span class="fmt-label" style="background-color: var(--m-color--accent-4);">Updated</span> **Progression**: Certain <a href="/manual/#triumphs-and-titles">Titles</a> now support Gilding by completing additional Triumphs
- <span class="fmt-label" style="background-color: var(--m-color--accent-4);">Updated</span> **Gear**: Shaders are now permanent unlocks and no longer consumable
- <span class="fmt-label" style="background-color: var(--m-color--accent-4);">Updated</span> **Gear**: Convert perks on Ghost Shells into <a href="/manual/#mods">Mods</a>
- <span class="fmt-label" style="background-color: var(--m-color--accent-4);">Updated</span> **Controls**: Add ADS Sensitivity Modifier for controller
- <span class="fmt-label" style="background-color: var(--m-color--accent-3);">Removed</span> **Destinations & Campaigns**: The Red War, Curse of Osiris, Warmind

<h2 variant="justify"><img m="icon" src="/assets/images/icons/expansion-witch-queen.png"> The Witch Queen <span part="suffix"><span style="opacity: 0.6;">v4.0.0 / </span>February 2022</span></h2>

- <span class="fmt-label">New</span> **Gear**: <i>Aggressive Frame</i> Scout Rifles
- <span class="fmt-label">New</span> **Enemies**: Lightbearing Lucent Hive
- <span class="fmt-label" style="background-color: var(--m-color--accent-4);">Updated</span> **Subclasses**: Arc, Solar, and Void 3.0 rework with Aspects/Fragments, Subclass Verbs
- <span class="fmt-label" style="background-color: var(--m-color--accent-4);">Updated</span> **Controls**: Add full auto toggle option to make all weapons full auto
- <span class="fmt-label" style="background-color: var(--m-color--accent-3);">Removed</span> **Destinations & Campaigns**: Forsaken

<h2 variant="justify"><img m="icon" src="/assets/images/icons/extra/expansion-lightfall.png"> Lightfall <span part="suffix"><span style="opacity: 0.6;">v7.0.0 / </span>February 2023</span></h2>

<h2 variant="justify"><img m="icon" src="/assets/images/icons/extra/expansion-the-final-shape.png"> The Final Shape <span part="suffix"><span style="opacity: 0.6;">v8.0.0 / </span>June 2024</span></h2>

- <span class="fmt-label">New</span> **Subclasses**: Prismatic (including new Super + Aspect for <img m="icon" src="/assets/images/icons/damage-void.png" alt="void" /> Titan, <img m="icon" src="/assets/images/icons/damage-arc.png" alt="arc" /> Hunter, and <img m="icon" src="/assets/images/icons/damage-solar.png" alt="solar" /> Warlock)
- <span class="fmt-label">New</span> **Subclasses**: Additional Aspect for <img m="icon" src="/assets/images/icons/damage-arc.png" alt="arc" /> Titan, <img m="icon" src="/assets/images/icons/damage-void.png" alt="void" /> Hunter, and <img m="icon" src="/assets/images/icons/damage-arc.png" alt="arc" /> Warlock
- <span class="fmt-label">New</span> **Gear**: <i>Support Frame</i> Auto Rifles, <i>Wave Frame</i> Swords
- <span class="fmt-label">New</span> **Enemies**: Dread faction
- <span class="fmt-label">New</span> **Enemies**: <a href="/manual/#banes">Banes</a>

- <span class="fmt-label" style="background-color: var(--m-color--accent-4);">Updated</span> **<a href="/manual/#hud">HUD</a>**: Standardize icons and priority of all buffs and effects, and split encounter and weapon effects into their own spaces to display more at once

<h2 variant="justify"><img m="icon" src="/assets/images/icons/expansion-edge-of-fate.png"> The Edge of Fate <span part="suffix"><span style="opacity: 0.6;">v9.0.0 / </span>July 2025</span></h2>

- <span class="fmt-label">New</span> **Portal** - Streamline multiple activity types into Ops categories with adjustable modifiers and standard <a href="/manual/#difficulties">difficulties</a>
- <span class="fmt-label">New</span> **<a href="/manual/#seasonal-hub-and-orders">Seasonal Hub</a>** - New weekly reward system
- <span class="fmt-label">New</span> **Gear**: <a href="/manual/#rarity-and-tiers">Tiered</a> Armor and Weapons
- <span class="fmt-label">New</span> **Gear**: <a href="/manual/#armor-and-stats">Armor 3.0</a> with major stat rework and set bonuses (existing armor mapped to new stats but no set bonuses)
- <span class="fmt-label">New</span> **Gear**: <i>Spread Shot</i> Hand Cannons, <i>High-Impact Longbow</i> Combat Bows, <i>Micro-Missile Frame</i> Pulse Rifles, <i>High-Impact Frame</i> Heavy Crossbows
- <span class="fmt-label" style="background-color: var(--m-color--accent-4);">Updated</span> **Combat Systems**: <a href="/manual/#ammo-generation">Ammo Generation</a> rework and Ammo Meter UI
- <span class="fmt-label" style="background-color: var(--m-color--accent-4);">Updated</span> **Combat Systems**: Melee damage scaling rework
- <span class="fmt-label" style="background-color: var(--m-color--accent-4);">Updated</span> **Power Level**: Reset all existing gear to level 10, new cap of 550
- <span class="fmt-label" style="background-color: var(--m-color--accent-4);">Updated</span> **Game Mode**: Power advantages disabled in Trials of Osiris
- <span class="fmt-label" style="background-color: var(--m-color--accent-4);">Updated</span> **Vault**: Access your Vault from anywhere

<h2 variant="justify"><img m="icon" src="/assets/images/icons/expansion-renegades.png"> Renegades <span part="suffix"><span style="opacity: 0.6;">v9.5.0 / </span>December 2025</span></h2>

- <span class="fmt-label">New</span> **Progression**: <a href="/manual/#seasonal-hub-and-orders">Orders</a> as a replacement for Bounties and Seasonal Challenges

<h2 variant="justify"><img m="icon" src="/assets/images/icons/extra/season-monument-of-triumph.png"> Monument of Triumph <span part="suffix"><span style="opacity: 0.6;">v9.7.0 / </span>June 2026</span></h2>

<p m="callout"><img m="icon" src="/assets/images/icons/item-ghost-reputation-rank.png"><strong>Author's Note</strong>: This is a free update and not a full expansion, but contains enough notable changes to be included.</p>

- <span class="fmt-label">New</span> **Pantheon 2.0**
- <span class="fmt-label">New</span> **Progression**: New set of Triumphs and Guardian Tenet vendors in the Tower with a ton of cosmetics, weapon engrams, and other special rewards
- <span class="fmt-label">New</span> **Subclasses**: Additional Aspect for <img m="icon" src="/assets/images/icons/damage-solar.png" alt="solar" /> Titan, <img m="icon" src="/assets/images/icons/damage-solar.png" alt="solar" /> Hunter, and <img m="icon" src="/assets/images/icons/damage-void.png" alt="void" /> Warlock, new <img m="icon" src="/assets/images/icons/damage-stasis.png" alt="stasis" /> and <img m="icon" src="/assets/images/icons/damage-strand.png" alt="stasis" /> Grenades, new <img m="icon" src="/assets/images/icons/damage-void.png" alt="void" /> Hunter Melee
- <span class="fmt-label">New</span> **Game Mode**: Sparrow Racing League
- <span class="fmt-label">New</span> **Gear**: <i>High-Impact Frame</i> Bolt-action Sniper Rifles
- <span class="fmt-label" style="background-color: var(--m-color--accent-4);">Updated</span> **Artifact**: Choose from 7 reprised Artifacts from past seasons with streamlined perk selection
- <span class="fmt-label" style="background-color: var(--m-color--accent-4);">Updated</span> **Gear**: Add catalysts to all Exotic Weapons that did not have one
- <span class="fmt-label" style="background-color: var(--m-color--accent-4);">Updated</span> **Gear**: All pre-<cite>Edge of Fate</cite> Raids, Dungeons, and Destinations now drop tiered loot with refreshed perks and set bonuses
- <span class="fmt-label" style="background-color: var(--m-color--accent-4);">Updated</span> **Gear**: Attune weapon drops for certain activities from your Inventory
- <span class="fmt-label" style="background-color: var(--m-color--accent-4);">Updated</span> **Game Mode**: <a href="/manual/#patrol-destinations">Patrol Destinations</a> now have Distortions with more powerful enemies and rewards
- <span class="fmt-label" style="background-color: var(--m-color--accent-4);">Updated</span> **Enemies**: All weapons can now stun Champions based on their Frame
- <span class="fmt-label" style="background-color: var(--m-color--accent-3);">Removed</span> **Portal**: Ops categories and Seasonal Hub have moved into the Destinations tab of the Director instead
