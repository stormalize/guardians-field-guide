## Beginning The Game

1. Create your character
2. New Light Quest - "A Guardian Rises"
3. !!! At the "sync with shaw's ship" step in the quest you have a choice to continue with the intro quest or skip it and go straight to the rest of the game by starting the "Skip New Light"
4. Optionally check out Ikora's premade builds over in the Bazaar in the Tower (quest name)

From here, the entire game is open for exploration. You can dive into the main sequence of campaigns or check out some routine missions.

## Other Activities

Explore ritual activities of all kinds, from protecting the system from threats to honing your skills against fellow Guardians.

- Patrol - public events
- Vanguard Ops - solo/fireteam/pinnacle
- Gambit - 4v4 PvEvP
- Crucible - PvP

### Guardian Ranks

Simple progression system that gradually gets you into more complex activities in the game.

Note that to get to guardian rank 3 you must do the intro mission for the Renegades campaign, which is a little annoying if you are wanting to do all campaigns in order.

## Pinnacle Activities / Endgame

Raids, dungeons

Most of these activities are open to you early on, but your chances of success are much greater if you first spend some time gearing up and getting more familiar with game mechanics and builds.

## Long-term Goals

- Complete exotic weapons and armor collection - some weapons may only be acquired through specific quests or chance to drop from a specific dungeon or raid
- Earn a Title
- Fashion
- Max out guardian rank 11
