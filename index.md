Welcome to the <b>Guardian's Field Guide</b>! This is a companion instruction booklet for Destiny 2, aimed at new and returning players to help you get up to speed and fill in some of the gaps of the new player experience as the game has been through many transformations over the years.

- [Guided Path](/guided-path/): Beginning the game, general recommended quest order, other activities, and endgame stuff to work towards
- [Manual](/manual/): Quick reference for how different parts of the game work such as gear, combat, progression, and more

## General Info

<p g="callout"><img g="icon" src="/assets/images/icons/item-ghost-reputation-rank.png"><strong>Author's Note</strong>: This section is a little more editorial and covers some common questions I see asked by new or returning players. I've been playing Destiny since the beginning and personally feel it is in a great spot and worth experiencing in its current state.</p>

<details g-layout="prose" prose-space="compact">
	<summary>What is the State of the Game?</summary>

The final live-service content update was <b>Monument of Triumph</b> on June 9th, 2026. From the announcement post about the final update:

> Though active development may be concluding, we will ensure that Destiny 2 remains playable, just as the original Destiny is today.
>
> <cite>[Destiny 2: Every End Is a New Beginning](https://www.bungie.net/7/en/News/Article/d2_may_21_2026)</cite>

The final update added a lot of requested great features and included a big refresh of rewards across almost the entire game. Without going into detail on every feature, Destiny 2 is in a very solid place and is a fun experience with a ton of hours of gameplay (even more if you want to dive deep into builds). The story context and new player experience is still a little rough around the edges but that is why I wrote this guide.

<details g-layout="prose" prose-space="compact">
	<summary>Is the Game Solo Friendly?</summary>

Yes, many of the missions in the game are made for 1-3 players including story campaigns, replayable missions, and dungeons. Dungeons have achievements for completing them solo, though that can be a significant challenge and you will need to go in very prepared with a decent build to succeed.

Auto-matchmaking is available for a fair amount of 3 and 6-player activities. Challenging endgame activities including Raids, Dungeons, and Conquests do not have matchmaking, although there is an in-game Fireteam Finder to try to find other Guardians looking to do the same content.

</details>

<details g-layout="prose" prose-space="compact">
	<summary>What Should I Do to Start?</summary>

There's really no wrong way to play Destiny 2. When you start a new character it will have you go through a couple introductory quests and then you are free to explore whatever you want.

You can work through the Story Campaigns, play some of the other repeatable PvE missions, or try out some PvP game modes. Over time you can focus on improving the quality of your gear, completing more difficult missions, collecting a bunch of Exotic weapons and armor to create different builds, and clearing endgame activities like Raids and Dungeons.

See the [Guided Path](/guided-path/) for more details.

</details>

<details g-layout="prose" prose-space="compact">
	<summary>Is My Gear Still Usable From When I Played Before?</summary>

For the most part, yes. Things like gear sunsetting and "New Gear Bonuses" have come and gone and we've gotten to a place with no artificial restrictions on what gear can be used across the game. Don't worry too much about Power Level of gear, it went through a reset in <cite>Edge of Fate</cite> and <cite>Renegades</cite> and as of the latest update is not as big of an obstacle as it used to be.

That said, over the years the game has gone through a couple armor/stat reworks and weapon power has steadily grown with new perk combos and features. <a href="/manual/#rarity-and-tiers">Tiered Weapons and Armor</a> (with <a href="/manual#armor-and-stats">set bonuses</a>) were introduced with the <cite>Edge of Fate</cite> expansion, and they are strictly better than older non-tiered gear.

Old good rolls are still good, but newer gear is a moderate upgrade that you can work towards while using your existing stuff. (Also, if you've got a lot of <a href="/manual/#weapon-crafting">crafted weapons</a> lying around, those can now be upgraded to Tier 5!)

See the <a href="/major-changes/">History of Major Changes</a> for a list of key changes from every expansion.

</details>
